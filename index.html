Good to know

A few answers.
Before you print.

Can I print a small quantity?+

Yes. Print & Go offers print-on-demand services. Tell us what you need and we’ll confirm the suitable process and quantity for your product.

How soon can my order be ready?+

Timing depends on the product, quantity, artwork and finish. Share your deadline when you enquire so our team can confirm what’s possible.

What if I’m not sure about the specifications?+

Start with the purpose and quantity. Our team can discuss the size, material and finish with you before confirming the job.
