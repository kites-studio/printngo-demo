Our story

Small jobs deserve
great attention, too.

Established in May 2004 by Jay Kok, Print & Go was built around instant printing and print-on-demand services.

From our shop at Wisma Central on Jalan Ampang, we help with the everyday materials that keep businesses moving: name cards, letterheads, envelopes, company profiles, brochures and leaflets.

Whether it’s a small quantity or a larger run, we’re here to work through the format, finish and timing with you.
